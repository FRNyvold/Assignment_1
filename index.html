  • Discworld: Adventures in Ankh-Morpork

    £40.00

    Based on Sir Terry Pratchett's beloved fantasy series, Adventures in Ankh-Morpork is the officially licensed Discworld tabletop roleplaying game. Set in the series' most enthusiastically self-interested city, Adventures in Ankh-Morpork invites you to tell stories where heroism, bureaucracy, and improbable coincidence collide with remarkable regularity.

    This starter set contains everything you need to set off on an epic adventure fraught with mild peril, interesting characters, and more bread than you can shake a scone at. You might even learn a thing or two along the way.

    The Discworld: Adventures in Ankh-Morpork Starter Set features:

    • A 36-page Rules Book that aims to introduce you to the wonderful world created by Sir Terry Pratchett.
    • A 44-page Adventure Booklet.
    • 8 pregenerated Player Character cards and 14 Non-Player character cards.
    • 10 Core cards and 24 Quick cards.
    • 4 delightfully double-sided player handouts.
    • A double-sided map displaying part of the city of Ankh-Morpork on one side, and the Sto Plains on the reverse.
    • A set of seven polyhedral dice.
